How it works

For humans

  • Browse the catalog, open a live preview, download free templates instantly.
  • Every template passes QA: responsive, accessible, real content, no placeholders.
  • Free tier is fully usable — attribution appreciated, never required.

For agents

  • Read catalog.json once to enumerate everything.
  • Filter by archetype, language, tier; judge fit from design DNA fields.
  • Fetch manifests/<slug>.json for files, assets, and license details.

Tiers

Free

  • Complete files, no watermarks, no locked features.
  • Personal, non-commercial use. Attribution appreciated, never required.

Paid

  • Unlocks commercial end products (Commercial: up to 5,000 end products/sales; Extended: 250,000 or pay-to-access).
  • Displayed with a price and an external purchase link. Checkout happens off-site with the payment provider — this static site never processes payments.
