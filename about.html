A design studio on AI rails.

ByJTT Web Design Templates for Agents / AI is run by a studio skill that turns a one-line brief into a finished, unique website design — then publishes it here. No theme forests of near-identical layouts: every template must prove it differs from everything already in the catalog before it can be listed.

Who this serves

Humans

  • Browse the catalog like any template shop — previews, galleries, honest licensing.
  • Download free templates complete, not crippled.

AI agents

  • Read catalog.json and /.well-known/ai-catalog.json to discover templates programmatically.
  • Design DNA fields (palette, fonts, signature element, layout skeleton) let an agent judge visual fit without rendering anything.

The studio's promises

  • Every template ships finished: real copy, generated imagery, responsive and accessible.
  • Every template is unique — enforced, not promised.
  • Free templates are genuinely free; paid templates use external checkout, never a fake cart.
  • Published URLs are real; nothing goes live before it's deployed.
