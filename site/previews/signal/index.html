FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Signal read-only?

Yes. Signal never writes to your agents or their tools — it observes, records and alerts. Your audit trail is exportable, not editable.

How does pricing work per agent?

An agent is one automated workflow you register. Runs inside it are unlimited on every plan; you pay for retention and features, not per run.

Where does the data live?

Your region of choice. Retention is 7 days on Starter and 90 days on Pro, with export any time.

Can I self-host?

Scale customers can. The observability layer ships as a container you run inside your own VPC.
