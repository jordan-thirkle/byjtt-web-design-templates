From green to ember

Every batch starts before the city wakes. We roast in 12-kilo runs so the drum — not a schedule — decides when a coffee is done. The beans then rest a full two days, letting the roast settle and the aromatics find their feet.

We buy from producers we can name, publish what we pay, and roast to the coffee, not to a formula.
